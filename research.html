Research

My research focuses on the integration of emerging electric loads, renewable generation, energy storage, and data-driven methods into modern power systems.

My work has evolved from photovoltaic system design and performance analysis toward broader questions involving microgrids, resilience, data centers, load behavior, and grid integration. Across these areas, I combine physics-based modeling, techno-economic analysis, machine learning, and power-system data analysis to study how energy systems can become more reliable, flexible, and sustainable.

Data Center Energy Systems

The rapid expansion of artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and high-performance computing is creating a new class of large and highly concentrated electric loads.

My current research examines how data centers interact with the electric grid, with particular attention to load behavior, energy supply, renewable integration, forecasting, and resilient on-site power systems.

I investigate questions such as:

  • How large data-center loads affect grid demand and resource planning.
  • How renewable generation and battery energy storage can reduce dependence on the bulk power system.
  • How grid-connected and islanded microgrids can support data-center reliability and resilience.
  • How different energy-system architectures compare in terms of cost, grid imports, emissions, and operational flexibility.
  • How emerging load patterns can be characterized and incorporated into utility planning and forecasting.

Microgrids & Energy Storage

A major theme of my research is the use of distributed energy resources, photovoltaic generation, and battery energy storage systems to improve grid flexibility and resilience.

My work includes both grid-connected and islanded energy-system architectures, with emphasis on the interaction between technical performance and economic feasibility.

Research topics include:

  • Renewable and hybrid microgrid design.
  • Photovoltaic and battery energy storage integration.
  • Peak-load management and grid-import reduction.
  • Resilience during grid disturbances and outages.
  • Energy management strategies.
  • Techno-economic optimization of distributed energy resources.
  • Grid-connected versus off-grid system architectures.

Photovoltaic Systems

Photovoltaics form the foundation of much of my earlier research and continue to be an important component of my work on microgrids and distributed energy systems.

My photovoltaic research has focused particularly on bifacial photovoltaic systems, irradiance characterization, performance monitoring, energy prediction, and energy management.

Key research topics include:

  • Bifacial photovoltaic systems.
  • Horizontal single-axis tracking systems.
  • Front- and rear-side irradiance.
  • Rear-to-front irradiance variability.
  • PV performance monitoring.
  • PV power and energy prediction.
  • Oversized photovoltaic arrays.
  • Maximum power point tracking.
  • Off-grid photovoltaic systems.

Data Analytics for Energy Systems

Data analysis is a cross-cutting component of my research rather than a separate application area.

I use computational and statistical methods to complement physical energy-system models, particularly when working with large operational datasets or complex relationships that cannot be captured adequately by simple analytical models.

My work includes:

  • Physics-informed modeling.
  • Machine learning and ensemble methods.
  • Explainable machine learning.
  • Time-series analysis.
  • Statistical analysis.
  • Load-pattern characterization.
  • Energy forecasting.
  • Data visualization.
  • Performance-model validation.

Research Approach

Although my projects span photovoltaics, microgrids, energy storage, data centers, and load forecasting, they share a common methodological framework:

Physical System Understanding → Data Analysis → Modeling → Validation → Engineering Decision Support

I am particularly interested in research problems where physical energy-system behavior and data-driven methods can be combined to support practical decisions in grid planning, renewable integration, system design, and energy management.
